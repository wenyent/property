      Good to know

      How is growth worked out?

      We average the $psf of every sale in the latest year (so far) and in the year five years earlier, then compare. $psf is price divided by floor area in square feet.

      Why do some developments not appear?

      A development needs at least one sale in both years within your size range. Those with few sales can show big swings, so check the sale count in brackets.

      What's included?

      Private condos and apartments, resale and sub sale only. HDB, ECs, landed homes, new launches and bulk sales are left out.

      How far is "near" the station?

      Distance is a straight line to the nearest exit, not walking distance. Only existing stations are included.

      Where does the data come from?

      URA transaction records and LTA station locations. "Last updated" at the top shows how fresh it is.

      Data: URA private residential transactions (resale and sub sale). Excludes HDB, ECs, landed homes, new launches and bulk sales. Growth compares average $psf in the latest year (year to date) with five years earlier.
